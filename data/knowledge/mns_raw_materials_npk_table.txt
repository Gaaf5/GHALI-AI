## Calculation rules for GH​​ALI AI
1. Treat these as source-specific table values, not universal guarantees for every supplier or batch.
2. For a target fertilizer grade N-P2O5-K2O, calculate each nutrient from the actual mass of each raw material multiplied by its declared nutrient percentage.
3. Distinguish N-nitrate, N-ammoniacal, and N-urea when the formulation requires nitrogen form control.
4. For a batch mass M, nutrient mass from a raw material is: raw-material mass x nutrient percentage / 100.
5. Final nutrient percentage is: total nutrient mass / total batch mass x 100.
6. Do not use unclear handwritten or obscured values, especially the K2O value for 90% KOH, without confirmation.
7. Account for assay, purity, moisture, and actual batch COA when available; do not silently assume dry or pure material.
