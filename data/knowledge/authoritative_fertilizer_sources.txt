Title: Authoritative References - Fertilizer Chemistry and NPK Manufacturing
Type: reference_index
Added: 2026-09-20

PURPOSE
This document is a curated source registry for GHALI AI. Prefer these sources when answering fertilizer chemistry, NPK manufacturing, product specifications, nutrient management, and industrial-process questions. Do not treat commercial marketing pages as primary evidence when an institutional or standards source is available.

TIER 1 - INTERNATIONAL / GOVERNMENT / TECHNICAL REFERENCES
1. FAO - Fertilizer Specifications and Plant Production and Protection Division.
Scope: fertilizer grades, nutrient expression, specifications for urea, ammonium sulphate, CAN, SSP, TSP, MOP, SOP, DAP and NPK complex grades including 15-15-15, 17-17-17 and 19-19-19.
URL: https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/thematic-sitemap/theme/spi/plantnutrition/fertspecs/en/

2. FAO - Fertilizer and Plant Nutrition Bulletins.
Scope: plant nutrition, fertilizer use efficiency, micronutrients, integrated nutrient management, fertilizer use by crop and phosphorus.
URL: https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/publications/fertilizer-and-plant-nutrition-bulletins/en/

3. IFDC / UNIDO - The Fertilizer Manual.
Scope: fertilizer production technology, raw materials, nitrogen, phosphate and compound fertilizer processes, physical properties, economics and industrial planning. IFDC describes it as a comprehensive reference for fertilizer production technology.
URL: https://hub.ifdc.org/handle/20.500.14297/1974

4. European Commission Joint Research Centre - BREF: Large Volume Inorganic Chemicals - Ammonia, Acids and Fertilisers (LVIC-AAF).
Scope: industrial production of ammonia, nitric acid, sulphuric acid, phosphoric acid, NPK fertilizers via nitrophosphate and mixed-acid routes, urea/UAN, AN/CAN and superphosphates; useful for process engineering and environmental/BAT context.
URL: https://eippcb.jrc.ec.europa.eu/reference/large-volume-inorganic-chemicals-ammonia-acids-and-fertilisers

5. International Fertilizer Association (IFA) - Nitrogen, Phosphorus & Potassium.
Scope: high-level chemistry and industrial origin of N, P and K fertilizer products and production chains.
URL: https://www.fertilizer.org/about-fertilizers/nitrogen-phosphorus-potassium/

6. IFA Technical Conference archive - NPK and compound fertilizer production.
Scope: technical papers on compound/complex NPK processes, granulation, raw materials, economics, process development and plant operation.
URL: https://www.fertilizer.org/resources/

7. USDA - Nutrient Management.
Scope: nutrient stewardship, fertilizer source/rate/time/place, nutrient losses and agronomic management.
URL: https://ers.usda.gov/topics/farm-practices-management/crop-livestock-practices/nutrient-management

8. NIST Chemistry WebBook / IUPAC-NIST databases.
Scope: chemical identifiers, molecular formulas, molecular weights and selected thermochemical/physical-property data; use NIST for chemical identity/property verification rather than fertilizer-grade guarantees.
URL: https://webbook.nist.gov/

SOURCE PRIORITY RULE
For fertilizer specifications and grades: prefer FAO or applicable national standard.
For industrial NPK process technology: prefer IFDC/UNIDO Fertilizer Manual, JRC LVIC-AAF BREF and IFA technical papers.
For chemical identity and molecular properties: prefer NIST/IUPAC.
For agronomic nutrient-management claims: prefer FAO and USDA/NRCS.
When sources disagree, preserve both claims, identify the source and publication context, and do not silently merge different standards.

IMPORTANT LIMITATION
The knowledge base stores curated summaries and source metadata, not full copyrighted books or manuals. GHALI AI should cite the source when using a claim and should retrieve the original publication for detailed engineering design or regulatory decisions.
