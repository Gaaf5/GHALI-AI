## Calculation rule for NPK formulation
For a batch of total mass M, if a raw material contains nutrient concentration C% and is the sole source of that nutrient, the required raw-material mass is:
Required mass = (Target nutrient % / Raw-material nutrient %) × Total batch mass.
Example: 1000 kg of 12-0-0 supplied entirely by urea (46% N):
Urea = (12 / 46) × 1000 = 260.87 kg.
Nitrogen supplied = 260.87 × 0.46 = 120 kg N.
Important: preserve the nutrient basis exactly as specified (N vs N-forms; P2O5; K2O), and account for assay/purity/moisture when applicable. Never silently assume a purity or dry basis.
