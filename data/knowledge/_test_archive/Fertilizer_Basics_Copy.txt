Title: Fertilizer Basics
Urea is CO(NH2)2 and is commonly used as a nitrogen fertilizer.
Urea contains about 46% nitrogen by mass.
Ammonium nitrate is a nitrogen fertilizer with formula NH4NO3.
Ammonium sulfate is a nitrogen and sulfur fertilizer with formula (NH4)2SO4.
DAP commonly refers to diammonium phosphate, (NH4)2HPO4.
MAP commonly refers to monoammonium phosphate, NH4H2PO4.
