# Fertilizer Formulation Rule: Single Nutrient Source
## Rule: Calculate the quantity of a fertilizer raw material
When a target nutrient percentage is supplied entirely by one raw material:
raw_material_mass_kg = (target_nutrient_percent / nutrient_percent_in_raw_material) * total_batch_mass_kg
The percentages must be expressed on the same basis. For example, urea contains 46% nitrogen (N).
## Worked example: 1000 kg of grade 12-0-0 from urea only
Inputs:
- Total batch mass = 1000 kg
- Target grade = 12-0-0
- Required nitrogen = 12% by mass
- Nitrogen content of urea = 46%
- All nitrogen must come from urea
Calculation:
- Urea mass = (12 / 46) * 1000
- Urea mass = 260.869565 kg, approximately 260.87 kg
Verification:
- Nitrogen supplied = 260.869565 * 0.46 = 120 kg N
- Final nitrogen percentage = (120 / 1000) * 100 = 12%
Mass remaining:
- Remaining mass = 1000 - 260.869565 = 739.130435 kg
- The remaining 739.13 kg must be a carrier, filler, or other approved material that contributes no nitrogen if the final product must remain 12-0-0.
## Important formulation controls
1. Do not silently assume that every remaining material is inert.
2. If another ingredient contains nitrogen, its nitrogen contribution must be included.
3. If urea assay, purity, or moisture differs from the nominal 46% N basis, use the verified effective nitrogen content instead.
4. Confirm whether the target grade is expressed on an as-is, dry, or another contractual basis.
5. Always verify the final total mass and nutrient contribution of every ingredient.
6. This rule calculates a direct single-source requirement; it is not a complete multi-ingredient formulation solver.
